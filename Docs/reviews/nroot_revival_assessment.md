## 3. Why integrating its output dismantled the banana eps^-2

The eps^-2 pole is produced by `monomialBasis`'s p=0 branch (master 1134–1146): a weight at ε^-1 over `x^(−1+Bε)` contributes `w/B` at ε^-2. So the pole coefficient is `Σ_i w_i/B_i` — a **nonlinear functional of the fitted roots that finitely many tower slices do not determine** unless the model order is right. The WIP introduced three sinks/movers for this content:

- **(a) Over-fit displacement on truncated towers (primary).** The per-offset confluent solve consumes slices k=0..N−1 *exactly by construction*; validation only has teeth on k ≥ N. Real towers' top epsilon orders are "partially combined" (master comment 843–847) and have shallow log depth, so an N=3..6 candidate can absorb the polluted offsets that stop the honest N≤2 run, achieve strictly larger `ValidOffsets`, and win under the unchanged max-ValidOffsets rule (master 1025–1027). `usableQMax = validOffsets − count − 1` then *includes* weight orders master would have salvaged with the loud "omitted … NOT trustworthy" warning; polluted weights flow into `1/B_i` with garbage roots. This is precisely "worked as a fit, broke on integration": every slice the fit was tested on reproduces, but the pole functional is wrong and the protective warning never fires.
- **(b) Integer snapping / clustering into the resonant sink.** `clusterRootSpecs` snaps roots to integers within 1e-2 and merges roots within relative 1e-2. Residual b's ride on `branchB`; a snapped root landing at exactly `r = −branchB` makes `basisB = 0`, and with `a+lp+1=0` that monomial enters the resonant path — where the WIP's change (4) converts the would-be `1/(Bε)` pole into a **finite** log term **with no warning** (master at least warned). Noisy near-zero roots similarly cluster into confluent `{0,p}` sectors whose ε-power is shifted up by p (pole content migrating from ε^-2 to ε^-1·Logx) and whose endpoint content is dropped in the limit path.
- **(c) Silent zero-weights defect.** In the WIP general `evaluateFit`, a failed `LinearSolve` writes **zero** weights (`coeffs[[i,q+1]] = If[solW === $Failed, 0, …]`) instead of invalidating the candidate — another silent content sink at exactly the orders that feed poles.

The Hadamard-FP change additionally perturbs cross-level pole accounting on its own (finite terms at L2 multiply lower-level Laurent series in the `run_ft_stepwise.m` chain), and its PV-pairing assumption between the two half-segments sharing a singular point is asserted, never enforced.
